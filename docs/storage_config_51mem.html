Variable selection

Variables were selected for their utility in human-centered benchmarking of high-impact phenomena (heat waves, cold air outbreaks, heavy precipitation), for general geoscience research use, and for driver analysis of the large-scale atmospheric patterns that condition those phenomena — blocking and Rossby wave activity (z_500, z_200), jet position and storm tracks (u_200, v_200, u_850, v_850), stratospheric variability and its surface coupling (u_50, v_50), and lower-tropospheric moisture and thermal structure (q_1000–q_850, t_1000–t_850).

Temporal aggregation follows the same logic: fields used for extremes and threshold exceedance are kept 6-hourly, while circulation diagnostics are stored as daily means.

Archive structure & format

3.10 PiB
Daily Initializations — full 2000–2025 archive

Forecasts are stored as one zarr store per model per initialization: forecasts_<model>/init_YYYYMMDDT00.zarr. Dimensions are (time, [number,] prediction_timedelta, lat, lon) for 6-hourly fields and prediction_timedelta_daily for daily aggregates; number is the ensemble-member dimension. All data are float32.

Daily aggregates are given by: means of the four 6-hourly values in the UTC calendar day; precipitation is the daily accumulation; 2 m temperature max/min are daily extremes.

Details may evolve as the archive continues to grow.
